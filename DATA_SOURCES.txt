Benchmark sources

MATH-500: the MATH-500 evaluation subset of the MATH benchmark.
MMLU-Pro: MMLU-Pro benchmark questions and answer options.
GSM8K: the GSM8K test split; the exact source URL is recorded in
analysis/data/manifest_v4.json.

Benchmark material and model responses retain their existing terms. This
archive does not assign a new license to third-party material. Dataset names,
question identifiers, model names, and research prompts are retained for
reproducibility. No model weights are included.

Upstream sources and license information:
- MATH: https://github.com/hendrycks/math
- MATH-500: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500
- MMLU-Pro: https://github.com/TIGER-AI-Lab/MMLU-Pro
- GSM8K: https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math

The repository's MIT license applies to original source code only; it does
not replace the upstream terms of benchmark content or archived responses.
